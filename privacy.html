Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing on its own. scrollwell.co has no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cookies and no forms. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address the way any web request does.

Booking a call

The booking button opens Cal.com. Whatever you enter there (name, email, notes) is handled by Cal.com under its own privacy policy and shared with Scrollwell so we can hold the call.

Email

If you email us, we keep the message and your address to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We never sell or rent contact details. If we contacted you first and you reply "no" or ask to be removed, we stop and keep only your address on a do-not-contact list so it stays that way.

Client work

Product photos, brand guidelines and ad account access a client gives us are used only to make that client's ads. Access to a client's Meta ad account is through Meta's partner access, never shared passwords, and the client can remove it at any time.

Your requests

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@scrollwell.co. We answer within 30 days.
